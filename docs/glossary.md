# Glossary

This glossary contains definitions for key terms used throughout the textbook. It is generated automatically.


## Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR)

The process of converting spoken language into written text.

## Autonomous Humanoid Pipeline

An integrated system enabling humanoid robots to perceive, understand, plan, and act autonomously based on high-level commands.

## Bipedal Locomotion

The act of walking on two legs, a complex challenge for robots requiring advanced balance and motion planning.

## Conversational Robotics

Robotic systems capable of engaging in natural, multi-turn dialogues with humans, understanding nuances, and providing relevant responses.

## Degrees of Freedom (DoF)

The number of independent parameters that define the configuration of a mechanical system, such as a robot's joints.

## Depth Camera

A camera that produces an image with depth information for each pixel, used for 3D perception.

## Digital Twin

A virtual representation of a physical object or system, used for simulation, analysis, and monitoring.

## Embodiment (Robotics)

The concept that an intelligent agent's physical form, sensors, and actuators, and its interaction with a physical environment, deeply shape its intelligence.

## Exteroception

Sensing of external stimuli; refers to a robot's perception of its external environment.

## Forward Kinematics (FK)

Calculates the end-effector's position/orientation given the robot's joint angles.

## Gazebo

A powerful 3D robotics simulator used for accurately simulating robots in complex indoor and outdoor environments.

## Human-Robot Interaction (HRI)

The study of interactions between humans and robots, focusing on design, understanding, and evaluation.

## IMU

Inertial Measurement Unit; a sensor that measures a body's specific force, angular rate, and often the orientation of the body.

## Inverse Kinematics (IK)

Calculates the joint angles required to achieve a desired end-effector position/orientation.

## Isaac ROS

NVIDIA's collection of GPU-accelerated packages that extend ROS 2 with high-performance perception and AI capabilities.

## Joint

A connection between two links in URDF, specifying their relative motion.

## Kinematics

The study of motion without considering the forces causing it, focusing on joint angles and end-effector positions.

## Launch File

An XML or Python file used to start and configure multiple ROS 2 nodes simultaneously.

## LiDAR

Light Detection and Ranging sensor; measures distance to a target by illuminating it with laser light and measuring the reflection.

## Link

A rigid body part of a robot in URDF, such as a limb or torso.

## Multi-step Action Generation

The process by which an AI system, often an LLM, translates a high-level goal into a sequence of atomic actions for a robot.

## Natural Language Understanding (NLU)

A subfield of AI that focuses on enabling computers to understand human language, including intent and context.

## Nav2

The standard ROS 2 navigation framework, providing global and local path planning, obstacle avoidance and recovery behaviors.

## NVIDIA Isaac Sim

A scalable robotics simulation and synthetic data generation platform built on NVIDIA Omniverse, offering realistic physics and high-fidelity rendering.

## NVIDIA Omniverse

A development platform for connecting and building 3D tools and applications, enabling universal interoperability across 3D workflows.

## Perception Pipeline

A series of processing steps that transform raw sensor data into meaningful information about a robot's environment.

## Physical AI

The study and development of artificial intelligence systems that interact with the physical world through a body (robot).

## Proprioception

The sense of the relative position of parts of one's own body and strength of effort being employed in movement; internal sensing in robots.

## Publisher

A ROS 2 node that sends messages on a topic.

## RGB-D Camera

A camera that captures both color (RGB) images and depth information per pixel.

## ROS Package

A fundamental unit for organizing ROS 2 code, containing source files, build scripts, and configuration.

## Series Elastic Actuators (SEAs)

Actuators that include an elastic element in series with the motor, providing compliance for improved force control and safety.

## Subscriber

A ROS 2 node that receives messages from a topic.

## Tactile Sensor

A sensor that measures pressure or force, providing a robot with a sense of touch.

## Unity

A cross-platform game engine used for developing 2D/3D games and simulations, including robotics and HRI.

## URDF

Unified Robot Description Format, an XML file format used in ROS to describe all elements of a robot.

## Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Systems

Robotic systems that integrate visual perception, language understanding, and action generation to interpret and execute human commands.

## VSLAM

Visual Simultaneous Localization and Mapping, using visual sensor data to estimate robot pose and build environment maps.

## Whole-Body Control

A control strategy for robots that coordinates all joints to achieve a desired motion while satisfying various constraints.

## Zero Moment Point (ZMP)

A stability criterion for bipedal robots; the point on the ground where the net moment of forces is zero.
