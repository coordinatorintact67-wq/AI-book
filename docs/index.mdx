# Welcome to the Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Textbook

This is the central hub for an open-source, living textbook on the exciting field of embodied intelligence.

## Our Vision

Our goal is to provide a comprehensive, practical, and cutting-edge resource for students, researchers, and hobbyists interested in building intelligent robots that can perceive, reason, and act in the physical world.

## How to Navigate This Textbook

The textbook is organized into several key modules, each covering a core area of Physical AI:

- **Module 1: The Robotic Nervous System (ROS 2)**
- **Module 2: The Digital Twin (Gazebo & Unity)**
- **Module 3: The AI Robot Brain (NVIDIA Isaac)**
- **Module 4: Vision–Language–Action (VLA)**

Use the sidebar to navigate through the chapters and sections.
